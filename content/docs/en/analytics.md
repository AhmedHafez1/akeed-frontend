## Overview

Analytics in Akeed help you answer one practical question:

**Are we shipping the right COD orders at the right time?**

Use your dashboard metrics to track confirmation quality, identify bottlenecks, and improve operational decisions week by week.

## The Metrics That Matter Most

Not every number has the same operational value. Focus on these first:

| Metric | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Confirmation rate | Shows how many sent confirmations convert into real, ship-ready orders. |
| Reply rate | Shows whether customers are engaging with your messages. |
| No-reply volume | Shows possible timing/message fit issues. |
| Failed sends | Highlights delivery or configuration issues. |
| Usage progress | Protects you from unexpectedly hitting plan limits. |

## Metric Definitions (Merchant View)

### Confirmation Rate

How many customers confirmed after message send.

High confirmation rate usually means:

- good customer intent,
- clear template wording,
- healthy timing setup.

### Reply Rate

How many customers responded (confirm or cancel) after send.

If reply rate is low, review template clarity and message timing before making big policy changes.

### Awaiting Response

Orders that were sent but still have no final customer response yet.

A temporary increase is normal during traffic peaks, but sustained growth means your follow-up/timing strategy likely needs adjustment.

### Failed Sends

Messages that could not be sent.

Track this daily. Even a small failure spike can quietly reduce your effective confirmation volume.

### Usage (Used / Limit)

Shows progress against your plan’s included confirmations.

Use this as an operations signal, not only billing info.

> [!WARNING]
> If usage reaches your limit, new confirmations may stop until renewal or upgrade.

## How To Read Trends (Weekly)

Use a simple weekly review:

1. Compare this week vs last week for confirmation rate and reply rate.
2. Check if no-reply volume moved in the opposite direction.
3. Check failed sends and usage pressure.
4. Make one controlled change (template, timing, or follow-up).
5. Review impact next week.

Do not optimize based on one day only.

## Suggested Alert Thresholds

| Signal | Suggested threshold | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reply rate drop | Down >10% vs prior period | Review template tone and send timing. |
| No-reply increase | Up >15% week-over-week | Enable/retune follow-up and escalation windows. |
| Failed sends | Sudden spike | Verify phone quality and settings. |
| Usage pressure | 80% and 95% | Plan upgrade discussion before limit hit. |

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Better approach |
| --- | --- |
| Looking only at total confirmed count | Track rates, not only raw volume. |
| Changing many settings at once | Change one variable per review cycle. |
| Ignoring failed sends because volume is small | Treat failures as early warning signals. |
| Reviewing data without date-range consistency | Compare equal periods (e.g., last 7 days vs previous 7 days). |

## Troubleshooting by Metric Pattern

### Reply rate down, failed sends stable

- Likely content/timing issue.
- Test a different template variant.
- Re-check quiet hours and first-send delay.

### Reply rate stable, confirmation rate down

- Customers are replying but canceling more.
- Review offer/expectation alignment and order quality upstream.

### No-reply up, usage normal

- Follow-up timing may be too late.
- First send may be missing customer active windows.

### Usage near limit with strong performance

- Good problem: demand is healthy.
- Plan capacity upgrade before sends stop.

## FAQ

### What should I optimize first?

Start with reply rate and confirmation rate. They are your strongest quality signals.

### How often should I review analytics?

Daily quick check, weekly decision review.

### Is high volume always good?

Only if quality holds. High volume with weak rates can hide expensive operational waste.

### Where do I improve outcomes after reviewing analytics?

Adjust automation timing and template style first.
