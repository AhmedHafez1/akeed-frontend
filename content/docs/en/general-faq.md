## Overview

This page answers the most common questions merchants ask before and after going live with Akeed.

If you are just starting, read [Getting Started](/en/docs/getting-started) first, then use this page as a quick reference.

## Setup and Integration

### Which platforms does Akeed support?

Akeed supports Shopify and can be connected to other commerce stacks through the available integration flow.

### How long does setup usually take?

Most merchants can complete the first setup in a few minutes, then send a test confirmation right away.

### Do I need technical work to begin?

For the standard Shopify path, setup is designed for non-technical teams. You can install, configure, and start confirming COD orders without custom development.

## WhatsApp and Messaging

### Do I need my own WhatsApp number?

No. Most merchants start using Akeed's shared official infrastructure. Later, larger teams can move to their own number if needed.

### Are messages sent through official APIs?

Yes. Akeed uses official Meta WhatsApp APIs.

### Can I customize the message text?

Yes. You can adjust confirmation copy and language style to better match your brand and customer tone.

## Order Confirmation Flow

### What happens when a customer confirms?

The order status is updated in your workflow so your team can prioritize shipping confirmed COD orders.

### What if the customer does not reply?

The order stays unconfirmed, and your team can hold it, follow up, or skip shipping based on your process.

### Does Akeed confirm online-paid orders?

No. The core flow focuses on COD order verification.

## Plans and Limits

### What happens when I reach my monthly confirmation limit?

New confirmations pause until your cycle renews or you upgrade the plan.

### Are there automatic extra charges after the limit?

No. You can choose to upgrade, but usage does not silently continue with surprise charges.

## Troubleshooting and Support

### Where should I start if messages are not sent?

Check auto-confirmation status, plan usage, and phone number formatting first. Then review [Troubleshooting](/en/docs/troubleshooting).

### When should I contact support?

Contact support when sends fail repeatedly, statuses look inconsistent, or your setup behaves differently than expected after basic checks.
