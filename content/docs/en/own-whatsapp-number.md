## Overview

By default, Akeed sends confirmations from its own official WhatsApp number, and you don't need to connect anything. You can also connect your own WhatsApp Business number, so customers get confirmations from your business name and number.

Akeed's number stays in place as a backup. Any message your number is not ready to send still goes out from Akeed's number, so customers keep getting their confirmations.

You can connect a number you already use in the WhatsApp Business app on your phone, or a number that is not on WhatsApp yet.

## Before You Start

- You need to be the store owner in Akeed. Only the owner can connect or disconnect a number.
- You need a Facebook account that manages your business, or you can create the business during the connection.
- If the number is in the WhatsApp Business app, update the app to its latest version and keep your phone nearby.

## Connect Your Number

1. Open **Settings**, then **WhatsApp**.
2. Choose **Connect your number**. A secure page opens in a new tab.
3. Finish the Facebook connection there: choose or create your business, then choose the number.
4. Return to Akeed. The WhatsApp settings refresh and show how ready your number is.

If the new tab does not open, allow pop-ups for Akeed in your browser and choose **Connect your number** again.

After you connect, Akeed prepares the message templates for your number and Meta reviews them. Until a template is approved, its messages keep going out from Akeed's number.

### If the Number Is Also in the WhatsApp Business App

You keep using the app on your phone to chat with customers one to one. Open the app regularly: Meta can disconnect a number whose app has been inactive for about two weeks. If that happens, confirmations go out from Akeed's number until you reconnect.

## When Your Number Sends

Your number sends a message only when both of these are true:

- the number is healthy: connected, with no payment or quality problem flagged by Meta,
- the exact template for that message is approved in the customer's message language.

Readiness is checked for each template and each language. Your confirmation in Arabic can be ready while the reminder in English is still in review. The WhatsApp settings list each template with its language, its status and the number that sends it now.

The settings also show the number that sent your last message. That tells you what happened before, not what happens next. The current sender of each template is what counts.

### When Akeed's Number Takes Over

Akeed's number sends instead of yours when:

- the template is missing in that language, still in review, rejected or paused by Meta,
- Meta flags a payment problem on your WhatsApp Business account or lowers the quality of your number, even when your templates are approved,
- your number is disconnected,
- Meta does not give a clear answer when Akeed sends from your number.

Your customer still gets the confirmation. The WhatsApp settings show the reason for each template.

### Getting Back to Your Number

Fix what the settings point to: wait for the template review, settle the payment in your Meta business account, or reconnect a disconnected number. Once the template is approved and the number is healthy, the next message goes out from your number again. You don't need to change anything in Akeed.

## What It Costs

### Prepaid Credits

1 credit equals EGP 1.

| Sent from | Cost per message |
| --- | --- |
| Your number | 1 credit |
| Akeed's number | 2 credits |

If your balance can't cover the full cost of a backup message from Akeed's number, the message is still sent and uses what is left.

### Shopify Plans

Once your number is connected, your plan has two separate allowances:

| Allowance | Size |
| --- | --- |
| Messages from Akeed's number | The messages included in your plan |
| Messages from your number | Three times the messages included in your plan |

Both allowances reset together at the start of each billing period. A message from your number uses the own-number allowance; when that is used up, it uses 1 message from the Akeed allowance. A backup message from Akeed's number uses the Akeed allowance; when that is used up, it uses 3 messages from the own-number allowance. When both are used up, sending stops until they reset.

### Meta's Charges

Meta charges your business separately for messages sent from your number, under Meta's own pricing and through your Meta business account. These charges are not part of your Akeed credits or plan. Messages sent from Akeed's number carry no Meta charge for you.

> [!INFO]
> Billing shows how many messages went out from each number, so you can compare the cost of both.

## Thank-You Replies

After a customer confirms or cancels, Akeed can send a short thank-you. In **Settings**, then **WhatsApp**, choose whether customers get a thank-you from your number. It is on until you turn it off, and your choice is kept if you reconnect.

- A thank-you from your number has no Akeed charge. Meta may charge your business under its own pricing.
- A thank-you from your number never falls back to Akeed's number. If your number can't send it, the customer gets no thank-you.
- Thank-yous from Akeed's number keep their own setting and cost, shown in your settings.

## Who Can Do What

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Connect, disconnect and reconnect the number, change thank-you replies, and see everything |
| Admin | Change thank-you replies and see everything |
| Viewer | See the number, its readiness and the costs |

## Disconnect Your Number

1. Open **Settings**, then **WhatsApp**.
2. Choose **Disconnect** and confirm.

From then on, confirmations go out from Akeed's number. Your orders and message history stay in Akeed.

> [!WARNING]
> To fully unlink a number that is also in the WhatsApp Business app, finish disconnecting in the app too. In the app, open **Settings**, then **Account**, then **Business Platform**, and tap **Disconnect Account**. The names may differ with your phone's language.

If you disconnect in the WhatsApp Business app first, Akeed notices it and switches your confirmations to Akeed's number.

## Reconnect Your Number

Once the WhatsApp settings show your number as disconnected, the owner can choose **Reconnect** and finish the Facebook connection again. Messages go out from Akeed's number until your number and its templates are ready again.
