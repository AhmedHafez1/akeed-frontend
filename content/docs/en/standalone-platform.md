## Overview

**Akeed — COD confirmation infrastructure for merchants.**

Akeed is not only for Shopify. If you sell on social media, through a marketplace, on another platform, or from a spreadsheet, you can use Akeed on its own. You add your cash-on-delivery (COD) orders to Akeed, Akeed confirms each one with the customer on WhatsApp, and you see the result in your dashboard.

This is called the **standalone** way of using Akeed. The confirmation flow is the same as on Shopify. Only the way orders arrive and the way you pay are different.

> [!INFO]
> Akeed is for COD order confirmation. It does not send confirmation messages for orders that are already paid online.

## Who It Is For

- Merchants who sell on Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp or through a call center.
- Merchants who get orders from a marketplace, a website or a platform Akeed does not connect to yet.
- Teams that keep their COD orders in Excel or Google Sheets.

## Two Ways to Add Orders

| Way | Best for | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Verify one order | A single order, or a quick test. | **Verify order** on the dashboard. |
| Import from a file | Many orders at once from a CSV or Excel file. | **Import from file** in the top bar. See [Bulk Order Import](/docs/bulk-order-import). |

Both ways send the same WhatsApp confirmation and use the same follow-up and no-reply rules. Imported orders appear in your **Confirmations** list together with every other order.

> [!INFO]
> Only owners and admins can add or import orders. Viewers can see orders and results but cannot change anything.

## Set Up in Three Steps

1. **Create your account.** Sign up and confirm your email address.
2. **Set up your store.** Enter your store name and your own WhatsApp number. Akeed sends messages to customers from its official WhatsApp number, so you do not need to connect a number.
3. **Try the message.** Akeed sends a free test confirmation to your own number so you can see what customers see. You can skip this step.

After that you are live. Add your first order or import a file.

New standalone accounts start with these defaults: the first message is sent right away, one follow-up goes out after 2 hours, and an order with no reply is marked **No reply** after 6 hours. You can change them in Settings. See [Automation Rules](/docs/automation-rules).

## How Credits Work

Standalone accounts do not use a monthly plan. You pay for what you use with **credits**.

| Rule | Details |
| --- | --- |
| One credit | One WhatsApp message sent to a customer. |
| Follow-ups | A reminder to a customer who has not replied uses one more credit. |
| Failed sends | If WhatsApp does not accept a message, the credit returns to your account automatically. |
| Launch credits | New accounts receive **30 free credits** once, with no credit card. Your balance is shown in the app. |
| Price | **EGP 2.00** per credit at the time of writing. A message and its follow-up cost EGP 4.00 together. |
| No subscription | You pay only when you buy credits. There is no monthly charge. |

### Buying credits

Open **Billing & credits**, choose how many credits you need, and continue to checkout. You can buy from 100 to 5,000 credits at a time, in steps of 50. For example, 500 credits cost EGP 1,000. Payment is handled by **Paymob**, the Egyptian payment provider, and you can pay by card or with Vodafone Cash. Prices are in Egyptian pounds and may change, so the price shown on the billing page is always the one that applies.

Akeed never asks for or stores your card or wallet details. Credits are added to your balance after Paymob confirms the payment.

> [!WARNING]
> When your balance runs out, new confirmations stop until you buy more credits. Watch your balance, and top up before sending a large import.

## What Is Different from Shopify

| | Shopify | Standalone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How orders arrive | Automatically from your store. | You add them, one by one or from a file. |
| Where results appear | Your Akeed dashboard and your Shopify order. | Your Akeed dashboard. |
| How you pay | A monthly plan billed by Shopify. | Credits bought through Paymob. |

Akeed does not change anything in another system for standalone orders. Use the status in your dashboard to decide what to ship.

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Better Approach |
| --- | --- |
| Starting a large import with a low balance | Check your balance first. Akeed will not start unless your credits cover every order in the import. |
| Shipping before the customer confirms | Ship **Confirmed** orders first. |
| Forgetting that follow-ups use credits | Plan for up to two credits per order. |
| Expecting online-paid orders to be confirmed | Only COD orders are confirmed. |

## FAQ

### Do I need a Shopify store?

No. Standalone accounts do not need Shopify or any other platform.

### Is Akeed different for Shopify stores?

Shopify stores install Akeed from Shopify, receive orders automatically and use a monthly plan billed by Shopify. Other merchants create a standalone account, add their own orders and use credits.

### Do I need my own WhatsApp Business account?

No. Akeed sends confirmations through its official WhatsApp infrastructure.

### Can my team share one account?

Yes. Owners and admins can add and import orders. Viewers have read-only access.
